{# ── Placement Prep Tips ── #}

🏢 Placement Preparation Tips

What top recruiters look for in campus hires.

💻

DSA & Coding Skills

Solve 100–150 LeetCode problems (Easy + Medium). Focus on arrays, strings, trees, graphs, and DP.

🗂️

Projects Portfolio

Build 2–3 end-to-end projects on GitHub. Recruiters value demonstrable work over grades alone.

📝

Resume Essentials

One-page resume: skills, 2+ projects, certifications, internships. Use action verbs and quantify results.

🎤

Mock Interviews

Practice on Pramp, Interviewing.io, or with peers. Do at least 5 mock rounds before the placement season.

🧮

Aptitude Tests

Many companies screen via Quant / Verbal / Logical aptitude. Dedicate 20 minutes daily to IndiaBIX or RS Aggarwal.

🌐

LinkedIn Profile

Complete LinkedIn with skills, projects, and certifications. Connect with alumni and recruiters in your target domain.

🤝

Soft Skills

Communication, teamwork, and problem articulation matter as much as technical skills in HR and managerial rounds.

🏆

Competitive Coding

Participate in CodeChef, Codeforces, or HackerEarth contests. A good rating is a strong differentiator on your resume.
